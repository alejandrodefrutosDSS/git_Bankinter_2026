# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I see usages related to self-hosted LLMs?
As long as Dataiku manages the inference, audit logs will include LLM usages. For self-hosted LLMs, you want to configure the connection [as such](https://doc.dataiku.com/dss/latest/generative-ai/llm-connections.html#locally-running-huggingface-models).

## What is the estimated cost for self-hosted LLM usages?
In this case, there is no cost estimation made by Dataiku.

## How can I configure the refresh frequency of my dashboards?
The input datasets are monitored on a very frequent basis (few seconds). Whenever the dataset is modified, the flow is built. Depending on the size of the logs and your partitioning strategy, it takes a few minutes to complete.
**Therefore, the refresh frequency is mainly driven by the frequency at which new audit logs are made available in your chosen storage.**
For Event Server, you can configure the **Flush interval (seconds)** and **Flush size (bytes)**. For example, by reducing the flush interval you will get faster refresh.

For Cloud logs, at the time of writing this documentation, the logs are made available on a daily basis.
