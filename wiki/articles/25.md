# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can I see usages related to self-hosted LLMs?
As long as Dataiku manages the inference, audit logs will include LLM usages. For self-hosted LLMs, you want to configure the connection [as such](https://doc.dataiku.com/dss/latest/generative-ai/llm-connections.html#locally-running-huggingface-models).

## What is the estimated cost for self-hosted LLM usages?
In this case, there is no cost estimation made by Dataiku.

## How can I configure the refresh frequency of my dashboards?
The input datasets are monitored on a very frequent basis (few seconds). Whenever the dataset is modified, the flow is built in a few minutes. Depending on the size of the logs (that you can control with partitioning), it takes few minutes to complete.
The refresh frequency is mainly driven by the frequency at which new audit logs are made available in your chosen storage.
For Event Server, you can configure the **Flush interval (seconds)** and **Flush size (bytes)**. You can reduce for example the flush interval to make audit logs available to the LLM Cost Guard solution on a more regular basis.
