The  **GenAI Cost Monitoring dashboard page**  provides a comprehensive  **overview of resource utilization and associated costs incurred by the usage of Large Language Models**  (LLM). It is designed to give project managers and finance teams insights into cost allocation, efficiency, and optimization opportunities.

### Cost and Usage Trends
 **Time-Series Analysis:**  Track the evolution of costs and LLM requests over time, broken down by individual projects, users, context, and more. These charts help to identify trends, peak usage times, and potential cost spikes.
### Cost-Efficiency Metrics
 **Estimated Savings Visualization:**  A specialized chart presents the estimated cost savings attributed to LLM mesh caching. This metric is calculated by comparing cached query hits to the total potential cost, highlighting the financial impact of utilizing cache mechanisms.
### Cost Distribution Insights
 **Cost Breakdown Pie Charts:** Understand the distribution of costs across various dimensions:
- By Project: View how costs are allocated among different projects.
- By User: Identify which users are incurring the most costs.
- By Usage Type: Distinguish between costs associated with EMBEDDING (text vectorization) and COMPLETION (text generation) operations.
- By LLM Type: Compare costs across different LLM types such as "OPENAI" or "AZURE".

Each segment of the pie charts includes interactive tooltips that provide the estimated cost savings for that particular segment.

In the dashboard,  **interactive filters are available** to refine data views based on date range, project key, user, LLM and many more, allowing for a customizable analysis experience.
