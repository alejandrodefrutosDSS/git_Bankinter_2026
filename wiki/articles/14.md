1. Import this project in your desired instance.
2. In the home page of the project, click on  **PROJECT SETUP** 
1. Choose the connection.
  - To use the logs of your instance, select **dku-audit-log**.
  - To use the anonymized demo logs included in this solution, leave the default connection **dataiku-managed-storage** as-is and go to step 5.
2. Click on the "Reconfigure" button to update the flow connections to match the selected one.
3. Click on the [compute_resource_usage_logs](dataset:compute_resource_usage_logs) dataset  to set the log path. Click on "Save" and go back to the Dataiku Application.
4. Optionally, toggle "Activate automatic night build"  to enable automatic overnight flow build with latest data.
5. Click on the **APPLY AND BUILD** button. It can take several minutes, depending on the number of logs available.
6. Open the  [LLM cost monitoring](dashboard:3NZ9w2C) dashboard.
