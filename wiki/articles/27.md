The solution has a built-in feature alerting people responsible for a project that its budget is completely consumed or close to be. The alerting takes the form of a mail sent to people configured in the [budget](dataset:budget) dataset. The alert is sent once after the budget overrun is detected. If a budget is changed to a higher amount in the [budget](dataset:budget) and the budget is exceeded again, then another notification will be sent.

#### Which alerts are available?
There are two types of alerts:
- project exceeded the budget
- project consumed more than 80% of the budget

#### How to configure recipients?
You can check the [Configuring budget rules](article:28) article.

#### How long does it take to receive the alert?
As we work with audit logs, this alert will be sent after some time and not on the spot right after the LLM usage that made the budget overrun.
Nevertheless, the elapsed time can be reduced to a few minutes with the right configuration. The main factor is the frequent at which the audit logs are made available. See [FAQ](article:25) (**How can I configure the refresh frequency of my dashboards and alerts?**) for more details.
