The solution has a built-in feature alerting people responsible for a project that its budget is completely consumed or close to be. The alerting takes the form of a mail sent to people configured in the [budget](dataset:budget) dataset. The alert is sent once in the lifetime of a project.

#### How to configure recipients?
- Go to [budget](dataset:budget) 
- In **projectOwner** or **operationalManager** columns, fill in the mail address of the person to alert.
If you wish to specify multiple people you can do so by concatenating mail addresses with the following character ```;```.
So for example: ```tim@mycompany.com;tom@mycompany.com;ben@mycompany.com;...```
